Formula Trace

Press Ctrl+Shift+M on a worksheet to apply the formula map. Press it again to remove the map. Set the palette under Map colors before applying it. Maps are snapshots of the sheet at the time of application.

From the grid, Ctrl+Q opens precedents and Ctrl+Shift+Q opens dependents. Up and Down switch to each referenced sheet and select its cell or block. Right traces one level deeper, and Left resumes at the reference you followed. Turn on Include formula components to navigate the expression tree. Double-click or press Ctrl+Q on the selected cell reference to drill further. Home selects the explored cell. Esc closes at the explored cell. Enter closes at the highlighted cell.

Use Ctrl+Backspace in the tree to go back. From the worksheet the default return key is Ctrl+Alt+B. Open Keyboard shortcuts in the pane to view or change worksheet bindings on supported Excel builds.
